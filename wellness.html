Wellness & AI Disclaimer

Last updated: March 2026

Lunations is a reflective journaling tool designed to support personal awareness and cyclical living. Please read this page carefully before using the app's AI-powered features.

Age Requirement

Lunations is intended for users aged 18 and older. See our Terms of Service for details.

Not Medical or Therapeutic Advice

Lunations is not a medical device, therapy platform, counseling service, mental health intervention, or substitute for professional care of any kind.

Nothing within Lunations — including AI-generated journal readings, astrological interpretations, moon phase information, Schumann resonance data, or Kp geomagnetic index data — should be interpreted as a medical diagnosis, treatment recommendation, clinical opinion, or professional advice.

AI-Generated Content

Lunations uses Anthropic's Claude AI to generate interpretive readings of your journal entries. These readings are:

  • Generated by artificial intelligence, not written or reviewed by a human professional
  • Interpretive and reflective, not diagnostic or prescriptive
  • Potentially inaccurate, incomplete, or not applicable to your situation
  • Not a substitute for the advice of a licensed therapist, counselor, psychologist, psychiatrist, or any other healthcare provider

AI readings reflect patterns in language, not clinical understanding. They do not "know" you, assess your mental state, or provide treatment.

Per Anthropic's API terms, journal text sent for AI readings is processed and not retained beyond the API request lifecycle. Your entries are not used to train AI models.

Astrological Content

Astrological information provided by Lunations — including natal chart positions, sign transits, compatibility overlays, and lunar cycle interpretations — is cultural, reflective, and interpretive in nature. It is not scientifically predictive of individual outcomes.

The research referenced on our Science page describes population-level findings from peer-reviewed studies. Individual experiences vary widely. No claim is made that the moon, geomagnetic activity, or astrological positions will produce any specific effect on any individual user.

Environmental Data

Schumann resonance and Kp geomagnetic index data are sourced from third-party providers and displayed for informational and self-tracking purposes. While we strive for accuracy, we do not guarantee real-time precision. This data is provided to support self-observation, not to diagnose or predict health outcomes.

Mental Health & Crisis Resources

Lunations is not a crisis service and is not equipped to identify or respond to mental health emergencies.

If you or someone you know is in crisis:

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988 (available 24/7 in the US)

Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741

International Association for Suicide Prevention: Crisis Centres Directory

Emergency services: Call 911 or your local emergency number

If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, psychotic symptoms, dissociation, or thoughts of self-harm, please close this app and contact a professional immediately.

Personal Responsibility

By using Lunations, you acknowledge that:

  • You will not use AI readings or app content as a substitute for professional advice
  • You are solely responsible for decisions you make based on information from the Service
  • Journaling can surface difficult emotions — if this becomes distressing, you are encouraged to seek professional support

No Professional Relationship

Use of Lunations does not create a doctor-patient, therapist-client, advisor-advisee, or any other professional relationship between you and Lunations, its developer, Anthropic, or any other service provider.
