What is Lunations

Your inner weather, mapped to the sky

A lunation is one complete cycle of the moon — 29.5 days of inner weather. Most apps show you the sky. Lunations helps you see yourself through the sky.

Log your energy, mood, clarity, and creativity every day. Get a personalized reading from your natal chart and the actual sky. After a few cycles, your patterns emerge. You start to know yourself in a new way.
